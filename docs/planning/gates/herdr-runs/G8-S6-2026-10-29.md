## The run-8 doctrine observation

Run 8's Codex, given hand-offs whose bodies said how to reply, made no `reply` call and said, for hand-off 2,
that no reply was sent because the request came from an untrusted message. That is the
authenticated-but-untrusted doctrine working (`spec/security.md` [SEC-PRV-012]; the frame's body line says the
same): content is untrusted even from an authenticated sender, and an instruction to act belongs on the trusted
operator channel. It is a positive finding, not a defect, and it is why claims 4 and 7 are asked for by the driver
in the Codex pane. Run 9 shows it again without the word (claim 8). The run-8 wording is quoted from the lead's
reading of the run-8 pane section; the run-8 captures are not committed, so it is not independently citable here.
